# ContentOS — Stakeholder Starter Guide

**Open it here:** https://sprout-contentos.vercel.app

A plain-language walkthrough for anyone testing ContentOS. No technical knowledge
needed. Keep this open while you click around.

---

## What ContentOS is, in plain terms

ContentOS is one place to create Sprout content, check its quality, and get it
approved. You tell it what you want. Behind the scenes it does three things for
you: it **writes** the content, **checks** it against Sprout's quality and brand
rules, and **flags** anything risky for a person to review before it goes out.

One promise it keeps: it will never make up product facts. Anything about Sprout
products has to come from GTM Studio. If it cannot confirm a product claim, it
marks it as "unverified" and asks a person to check, instead of guessing.

## Try this first (about 5 minutes)

| Step | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open the **Dashboard**. The cards at the top show what needs attention. |
| 2 | Click **My Jobs**. Four example jobs are already there. Try the **Table / Board / List** buttons on the right. |
| 3 | Open **"Why Philippine SMEs lose days to manual payroll."** |
| 4 | You land on **QA Review**: the content is on the left, the quality feedback is on the right. |
| 5 | Find the red **UNVERIFIED** flag. That is the system catching a product claim it could not confirm. |
| 6 | Click **Accept** on a suggestion and watch the content update on the left. |
| 7 | At the bottom, click **Accept All Changes**, then **Submit to Final QA** to see the score change. |
| 8 | Open the **DOLE regulatory** job. It is high-risk, so it was sent for human approval automatically. |
| 9 | Open **Human Review Queue**, click **Review**, read the summary, and click **Approve**. |
| 10 | Click **QA Check** (the teal button) to score your own pasted or uploaded content. |

## Starting a New Content Job

| Step | What you do |
|---|---|
| **1. Choose the workflow** | **New Content** (write something new) or **Content Repurposing** (reuse an existing asset). |
| **2. Choose the type** | Pick a card (Blog, Email, Social Post, etc.). Each card shows the expected length and how long it takes to set up. |
| **3. Fill the brief** | Only the essentials show first. Audience, content, and advanced options are in sections you open as needed, so nothing is overwhelming. Use the **+ / −** stepper to choose how many pieces you want. |
| **4. Submit** | Click **Submit to ContentOS**. It creates the content, checks it, and opens the job for you. |

## QA Check (score any content on demand)

| What you see | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Upload a file** (main option) | Drag and drop, or click to choose a file (Word, PDF, text, or Markdown). |
| **Paste content** | Paste text in directly instead of uploading. |
| **Analyze a URL** | Check a page that is already live. |
| **Product** (optional) | If you pick a Sprout product, it also checks that product claims are accurate. |
| **Run QA** | Scores the content and opens the results. |

This does not create a content job. It is just a fast quality check.

## Opening a job (the tabs)

| Tab | What you see |
|---|---|
| **QA Review** | The content next to its quality feedback. This is where most of the work happens. |
| **Agent Outputs** | The plan and reasoning behind the content (audience, narrative, claims). |
| **Brief** | The original request. |

done and what is next (for example, "quality check done, waiting for approval").

## QA Review screen (the most important one)

**Left side = the content. Right side = the feedback.**

| Button or item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Current / Original toggle** | Compare your working version against the first draft. |
| **Click any block of text** | Edit it directly. |
| **Scorecard (8 checks)** | A score for each quality area (clarity, brand voice, accuracy, product claims, and more). Click one to see details. |
| **Product Validation** | Shows whether product claims were confirmed against GTM Studio. |
| **Compliance Validation** | Shows whether the content is sensitive and needs legal or compliance care. |
| **Accept** (on a suggestion) | Applies the fix; you see the change appear on the left. |
| **Reject** | Keeps the content as is. |
| **Edit** | Lets you write your own version of the fix. |
| **Human Review** | Sends that one item to a person to decide. |
| **Accept all [area] fixes** | Applies every fix in one category at once. |
| **Accept All Changes** (big green, very bottom) | Applies every suggested fix at once. |
| **Submit to Final QA** | Re-scores the content after your edits. |
| **Export** | Moves to the download/publish step. |

## Human Review Queue (built for non-technical reviewers)

Each item is a simple card.

| What you see | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Card title, type, risk level** | Tells you what the content is and how sensitive it is. |
| **Reason** | Why this needs a human (for example, "product claims detected"). |
| **Recommended action** | What we suggest you do. |
| **Review** | Opens the full review. |

When you click **Review**:

| What you see | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Content (left)** | The piece you are reviewing. |
| **Review summary (right)** | Answers four questions: Why am I reviewing this? What is risky? What should I verify? What do we recommend? |
| **Approve** | Approves the content (now ready to publish). |
| **Request Revision** | Sends it back with your notes. |
| **Edit Content** | Opens the editor to fix it yourself. |
| **Reject** | Stops the content. |
| **Show advanced details** | Reveals extra technical detail only if you want it. |

## A few things that decide what happens

| Term | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Risk level (Tier 0, 1, 2)** | How sensitive the content is. Tier 2 (regulatory, competitor, pricing, security, or executive content) always needs a person to approve it before it ships. |
| **Quality score** | Out of 5. High scores pass automatically; medium scores ask for edits; low scores or any serious issue get held for a person. |
| **GTM Studio rule** | Product facts only come from GTM Studio. Anything it cannot confirm is flagged, not published. |

## Good to know (this is an early prototype)

| Point | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Data resets** | The example jobs and anything you create are temporary and reset over time. Create freely; nothing is permanent yet. |
| **The AI is simulated** | The content and scoring are realistic demonstrations, not yet powered by a live model. |
| **Roles are a preview** | The Standard/Admin switch shows different views; it is not a real login. |
| **Exports are previews** | Nothing is actually sent to Google or HubSpot yet. |

None of this affects what you are evaluating: the flow, the guardrails, and the
experience.

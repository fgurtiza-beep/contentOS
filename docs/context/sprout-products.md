## **Product: Sprout HR**

**One-Line Description:** A People-first AI platform for end-to-end HR, built specifically for Philippine businesses to manage people, payroll, and compliance in one connected system.

**The Problem It Solves:** Philippine businesses struggle with fragmented HR and payroll systems, recurring payroll errors, manual timekeeping, and constant compliance anxiety (DOLE, BIR, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG). HR teams are stuck firefighting admin work, payroll officers deal with cutoff crises, and leaders lack unified workforce visibility for decision-making.

**Core Features:**

* **Digital Employee Hub** \- Centralized employee records, documents, employment data, and workforce profiles in one unified system of record.  
* **Timekeeping to Payroll Sync** \- Enterprise-grade payroll built specifically for Philippine labor laws, with automated computations, government contributions, and statutory reporting.  
* **Multi-Location HR & Shift Management \-** Automated shift scheduling, attendance tracking, overtime, night differential, holiday pay, and complex workforce rules synced directly to payroll.  
* **Mobile App (Employee Self-Service)** \- Full-featured mobile access for employees and managers to clock in/out, file leave, view payslips, approve requests, and manage schedules anytime, anywhere.  
* **Built-In Performance Appraisals** \- Standardized performance appraisals embedded directly inside Sprout HR — reviews, evaluations, and progress tracking are synced to employee records without needing a separate system.  
* **Embedded Financial Wellness** \- Integrated financial empowerment tools that support employee well-being — reducing stress, improving focus, and strengthening retention while tied directly to payroll data.  
* **Embedded Employee Referrals** \- Built-in employee referral functionality to activate internal networks, streamline candidate sourcing, and track referral performance within the HR dashboard  
* **Built-in AI** \- Embedded AI assistance for HR queries, compliance checks, and predictive workforce insights — designed specifically for Philippine business rules.  
* **Role-Based Access Controls** \- Granular employee, manager, HR admin, and payroll admin permissions to ensure governance and data security.  
* **Automation & Customizations** \- Extend system capability through plugins, RPAs, FDEs, and automation insights — adding intelligence without adding complexity.

**Key Benefits (Outcomes):** 

* Run timekeeping without errors or disputes \- 98% faster  processing, 70% fewer disputes, and a 90% discrepancy reduction  
* Run your business with confidence on a single, unified system of record. With payroll and time at the core, data stays consistent across HR, Finance, and Operations — eliminating manual reconciliation, fragmented records, and costly errors.  
* Empower employees to manage payslips, attendance, requests, and benefits in one seamless flow. With AI-powered support from Sidekick, HR teams handle fewer tickets, approvals move faster, and everyone gains clear visibility into every request.  
* Stay compliant without the stress. Sprout automates 100+ labor rules — reducing penalties, audit risks, and reputational exposure while keeping every report accurate and audit-ready.  
* Start with what you need today and expand as you grow. With one shared data layer at the core, you can extend the platform through integrations, automation, custom workflows, or managed services — without disrupting operations.  
* Adapt your processes as your organization evolves. Extend capabilities through automation, plug-ins, and custom workflows — gaining flexibility and control without introducing more tools to manage.

## **Product: ReadyCash**

**ONE LINE DESCRIPTION:** A salary advance benefit embedded in the Sprout HRIS that gives Filipino employees instant access to emergency cash, with automatic payroll deductions for repayment and zero cost and minimal admin work for employers.

**THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES:** Employees face financial emergencies between paydays but have nowhere fast and affordable to turn. Traditional loan providers take days to approve, charge high interest rates, and require extensive documents. The downstream effect for employers is a workforce that shows up financially stressed, disengaged, and more likely to leave.

**CORE FEATURES:**

* **Instant cash disbursement**: No approval period; funds land in the employee's payroll bank account in 2–3 minutes  
* **Multiple applications within one credit limit**: Employees can apply more than once as long as they're within their approved limit, with no waiting period between applications  
* **Automated payroll deduction repayment**: Repayments are automatically deducted bi-monthly via payroll; no manual tracking required  
* **Smart credit limits**: Limits are set based on the employee's average net pay and repayment ability  
* **Built into Sprout HRIS**: Accessible directly through the Sprout HR portal, on both mobile and web  
* **Admin dashboard**: Real-time loan monitoring for HR and payroll officers

**KEY BENEFITS:**

* Employees get emergency funds in minutes, with no lengthy paperwork or waiting  
* Employers reduce absenteeism, disengagement, and turnover at zero cost to the business  
* No impact on company cash flow or balance sheet as loans are fully funded by Netbank, not the employer  
* Payroll reconciliation stays intact since deductions are automated and aligned with existing payslip schedules
